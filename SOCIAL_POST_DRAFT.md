### Option 1: LinkedIn / Professional Post (Recommended)

Contract review software usually produces the same output: bulleted summaries that miss compounding legal risks.

For Kritical Hacks 2026, we built Veritas AI: a legal document intelligence and investigation workstation.

Tagline: "Don't Just Read the Contract. Investigate It."

Veritas AI does not simply read text. It investigates agreements across multiple forensic and analytical layers:

1. Evidence-Grounded Clause Auditing: Every identified risk is grounded in verbatim quotations verified against the document byte stream, structured across 13 operational fields.
2. Cross-Clause Interaction Reasoning: Correlates separate sections, such as a trailing one-month liability cap paired with an uncapped, unconditional customer indemnification covenant.
3. Deterministic Arithmetic: Calculates exact calendar non-renewal notice deadlines, annualizes payment interest rates without compounding assumptions, and computes effective liability recovery ceilings.
4. Four-Tier Protections Audit: Eliminates false negatives by recognizing partial and asymmetric clauses (like one-sided consequential damages waivers).
5. Document Integrity and ELA Forensics: Analyzes PDF dictionary structures, timestamp discrepancies, and 15x amplified Error Level Analysis compression maps.
6. Permanent Binary Redaction: Cleanses sensitive personal data (PAN, Aadhaar, bank records) directly from the underlying PDF stream using PyMuPDF.
7. Autonomous Counterparty Investigation: Deploys TinyFish autonomous web agents to verify stated legal entities and corporate registration numbers (CIN, GSTIN) against public registries.
8. Interactive Bilateral Redrafting: Generates balanced contractual drafting and tactical negotiation recommendations.

Tech Stack: Next.js 16 (React 19, Tailwind CSS), FastAPI, PyMuPDF, ReportLab, and the official TinyFish SDK.
